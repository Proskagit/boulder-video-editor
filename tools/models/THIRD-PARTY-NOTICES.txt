THIRD-PARTY NOTICES - AiVideoEditor 1.0, AI models archive
==========================================================

This archive holds the AI models AiVideoEditor uses locally (DECISIONS.md D066 par. 3). They are ONNX conversions of the
third-party models below, made for AiVideoEditor by the recipes named; the weights are the original authors' work and stay
under their licences. AiVideoEditor never downloads them; Install-Models.cmd copies them into
%LOCALAPPDATA%\AiVideoEditor\models after checking every file's size and SHA-256 (models.json).

The licence texts are in licenses\.
