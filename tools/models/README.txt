AiVideoEditor 1.0 - AI models
=============================

These models make the AI features of AiVideoEditor work:
  sam2.1-large  - «Разметить зацепки» (hold markup)
  people-v2     - Follow and Occlusion (the climber followed by the frame, the hold outlines hidden behind people)
  audio-v1      - voice enhancement and ducking under speech

Installing
  1. Unpack this archive anywhere.
  2. Double-click Install-Models.cmd. It copies the models into %LOCALAPPDATA%\AiVideoEditor\models and checks every
     file (about 1.2 GB of disk space is needed).
  3. Start AiVideoEditor (restart it if it was running).

Without the models AiVideoEditor works, only the features above say that their model is missing.
The archive can be deleted after installing. The notices and licences of the models: THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt, licenses\.
